Support

MACROcosm is a calorie and macro tracker that measures your target from your own weigh-ins and logs.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/hguha/tracker-apps/issues and include your app version from Settings → Data & sync. Please don't post your email address or food log there.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Choose “Use this device only” and everything stays on your phone. Sign in later to sync; your log moves onto the account.
  • I didn't get a sign-in code. Check spam, wait a minute, then tap resend. Codes expire after an hour and work once.
  • Why did my calorie target change? Once you have a week with at least four logged days and three weigh-ins, the weekly check-in measures what you actually burn and adjusts the target, by at most 150 kcal a week.
  • Reminders don't arrive. Allow notifications for MACROcosm in your phone's Settings. Each reminder only fires if that meal or weigh-in is still missing.
  • Export or delete my data. Settings → Data & sync → Export saves a backup. Account → Delete account removes your account and every row of it from the server.
