Support

REPutation is a workout tracker that logs sets fast, tracks personal records and progress, and works offline with no account.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/hguha/tracker-apps/issues and include your app version from More → Data & sync. Please don't post your email address or training log there.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Choose “Use this device only” and everything stays on your phone. Sign in later to sync; your history moves onto the account.
  • I didn't get a sign-in code. Check spam, wait a minute, then tap resend. Codes expire after an hour and work once.
  • How are personal records counted? A set is a record when it beats your best estimated one-rep max, weight or reps for that exercise. Record badges count the sessions where you set at least one.
  • Reminders or the rest timer don't alert me. Allow notifications for REPutation in your phone's Settings. Workout reminders only arrive on days you haven't trained yet, and stop once the week's goal is met.
  • Export or delete my data. More → Data & sync → Export saves a backup. More → your account → Delete account removes your account and every row of it from the server.
